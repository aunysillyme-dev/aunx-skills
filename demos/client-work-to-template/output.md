# Campaign review method

## Purpose

Compare observed responses fairly and choose the next message test with a documented reason.

## Produces

A review table and a decision record that can be reused for a new engagement.

## Principles

Use observed reach as the denominator when comparing responses. Audience size alone does not establish how many people saw a message. Treat a response rate from limited observations as uncertain.

## Steps

- Collect observed reach and responses for each message from the authorized reporting source.
- If observations are incomplete or incomparable, gather comparable evidence before ranking.
- Calculate response rate as responses divided by observed reach. If reach is absent or zero, mark the rate unavailable and investigate the source.
- Choose a minimum evidence requirement using the next engagement's uncertainty tolerance and reporting quality; record the reason rather than inheriting a cutoff.
- If the evidence meets that requirement, compare response rates and uncertainty. Otherwise, choose the next evidence-gathering action.
- Record the next test, decision reason, evidence source, and what would change the decision.

## Templates

| Message label | Observed reach | Responses | Response rate | Uncertainty | Next action | Decision reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [label] | [reach] | [responses] | [responses / reach] | [evidence limits] | [next test or measurement] | [why this action follows] |

Decision record: [chosen action], [evidence supporting it], [evidence requirement], [what would change the decision].

## Failure modes

- Ranking on audience size can favor messages few people saw. Compare the denominator to the question before calculating the rate.
- Sparse observations can make a rate look stable. Record uncertainty and gather more evidence when it could change the next action.

## Boundaries

This method supports campaign review with comparable observations. It does not establish causation or guarantee results. Fresh reporting evidence and an engagement-specific evidence requirement are needed for every use.
